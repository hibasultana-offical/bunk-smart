# 🎓 Bunk Smart — Stay Above 75%

### Your Attendance. Your Skills. Your Time. Your Future.

**Bunk Smart** is a student-focused academic management and productivity platform designed for students who struggle to balance college attendance, academic responsibilities, and personal skill development.

College life is not just about attending lectures. Students also need time to learn programming, explore new technologies, build projects, participate in hackathons, and develop skills for their future careers.

But managing attendance while making time for these activities can be challenging.

**Bunk Smart aims to make that balance easier.**

It helps students understand their attendance, monitor academic progress, plan their schedules, and make more informed decisions about how they use their time.

Our goal is simple: Help students stay academically responsible without losing sight of their personal growth.

---

## 💡 The Problem We're Solving

Many college students face common challenges:

- They struggle to maintain the minimum required attendance.
- They don't always know how many lectures they can afford to miss.
- They find it difficult to track attendance separately for every subject.
- They spend time calculating attendance percentages manually.
- They struggle to balance college schedules with coding, learning, projects, and other skill-development activities.
- They often lack a centralized place to manage their academic routine.

Students should not have to choose between attending college responsibly and developing skills beyond the classroom.

**Bunk Smart is built to help bridge that gap.**

---

## 🚀 What is Bunk Smart?

Bunk Smart is an attendance tracking and student productivity management platform that brings essential academic tools together in one place.

It aims to help students:

- Monitor subject-wise attendance.
- Keep track of attended and missed lectures.
- Maintain the required attendance percentage.
- Understand how many consecutive lectures they need to attend to reach their target.
- Organize their timetable and academic calendar.
- Monitor attendance trends and academic progress.
- Make better use of their available time for skill development.

The default attendance target is **75%**, with the flexibility to support different institutional requirements.

## 🏗️ How It Works

Bunk Smart follows a simple frontend-backend architecture:

1. The student interacts with the dashboard.
2. JavaScript communicates with the Flask backend through API endpoints.
3. Python handles application logic and attendance calculations.
4. SQLite stores subject and attendance information.
5. The backend returns data in JSON format.
6. The dashboard can use this data to display updated academic information.
